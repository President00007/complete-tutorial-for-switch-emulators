# Welcome to my tutorials!
These are tutorials I made for people to install a Nintendo Switch 1 emulator of their choice! I tried my best to make them as simple to understand and visually pleasant as possible!
> [!WARNING]
> I want to clarify that in none of these there's piracy involved, nor my goal is to rob people's awesome projects like these. What I'm doing is solely providing an encyclopedia to guide you between different options to be able to play Switch games you already own on your PC, giving the choice for the best option on you! :D

With that being said, here's all different tutorials I've made:

## TUTORIALS WITHOUT NEXTENDO

Sometimes, people prefer to just play offline games, and for those cases, that's why I made this little section! Feel free to take a look!

## TUTORIALS WITH NEXTENDO
Nextendo is a new project that focuses on adding custom online servers connectivity, and not only it's compatible between Ryujinx and Citron, but it also unlocks compatibility with real, modded switches! It's in continuous improvement day after day! Unfortunately, it's not available for every game (yet) but it covers most main-stream ones (Splatoon 3, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe...)
